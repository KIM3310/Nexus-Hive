Privacy

Nexus Hive is a public governed analytics demo that uses synthetic sample data. Do not submit customer data, credentials, production SQL, or private business records through the public surface.

The public static site may be used with contextual advertising and aggregate, privacy-safe measurement after deployment. Query workbench data, saved SQL, exports, dashboards, and private connector work remain outside advertising surfaces.

Private implementation work is handled through a separate inquiry and service process with its own data boundaries.
